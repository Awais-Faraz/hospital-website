About CareNova Hospital

A hospital built around one idea: people should understand their own treatment.

Our story

CareNova Hospital opened in 2008 with a small outpatient clinic in Your City. Today we are a 150-bed multi-specialty hospital with an emergency department, operation theatres, a modern laboratory and imaging services.

We keep the same promise we started with: honest advice, fair pricing and respect for every patient.

Our mission

To provide safe, affordable and compassionate healthcare for families in Your City and the surrounding districts.

What we stand for

  • Safety first: infection control, patient identification and checklists in every ward
  • Clear communication: your doctor explains the diagnosis and options in simple language
  • Fair cost: estimates are shared before major procedures
  • Privacy: your records are kept confidential

Facilities

  • 24/7 emergency and ambulance
  • ICU, CCU and neonatal unit
  • Operation theatres
  • Laboratory, X-ray, ultrasound, CT scan
  • In-house pharmacy and physiotherapy

# Hospital SEO Guide (Off-page + ongoing work)

## A. Launch checklist (first week)
1. Buy domain, enable HTTPS, upload site.
2. Google Search Console: add property, verify, submit `https://yourdomain/sitemap.xml`, request indexing for home + department pages.
3. Google Analytics 4 (or Plausible) + track clicks on phone and WhatsApp buttons.
4. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console.
5. Test: PageSpeed Insights (aim 90+ mobile), Rich Results Test (schema), Mobile-Friendly check.

## B. Google Business Profile (most important for a hospital)
- Create/claim the profile, category "Hospital" (+ secondary: Emergency room, specialties).
- NAP must match the website exactly (same name, address, phone).
- Add hours (24/7), photos (building, ER, wards, team), services, appointment link, website link.
- Ask real patients for Google reviews and reply to every review politely (never share patient medical details in replies). Never buy fake reviews.
- Post weekly updates (camps, new doctor, health tips).

## C. Off-page SEO
**Local citations (same NAP everywhere):** Marham, Oladoc, Sehat.com.pk, Dawaai, Yellow Pages Pakistan, Facebook page, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Apple Maps, Bing Places.
**Backlinks (quality over quantity):**
- Press release / local news for new equipment, free medical camps, awareness days
- Doctors write guest articles on health sites and link to department pages
- Partnerships: local universities, nursing schools, NGOs, corporate panels, insurance company hospital lists
- Doctors' profiles on Marham/Oladoc linking to your site
- Health directories and Pakistan medical association listings
**Social + content:** share every blog post on Facebook/Instagram/YouTube; short doctor videos (symptoms, myths, prevention) in Urdu and English.
**Avoid:** paid link farms, comment spam, PBNs, fake reviews. They can cause a Google penalty.

## D. Content plan (E-E-A-T for medical sites)
- Publish 2-4 articles per month; each reviewed by a named doctor ("Medically reviewed by Dr. X, MBBS, FCPS").
- Target patient questions: "chest pain causes", "best cardiologist in [city]", "normal delivery cost in [city]".
- Add doctor pages with PMDC registration number, qualifications, photo.
- Update old posts every 6 months and change the date.
- Add real patient stories only with written consent.

## E. Keywords to target (replace [city])
best hospital in [city], emergency hospital [city], cardiologist in [city], gynecologist in [city], orthopedic surgeon [city], children specialist [city], neurologist [city], 24 hour hospital [city], hospital near me.

## F. Monthly routine
Check Search Console (queries, errors) -> fix broken links -> add 2-4 blog posts -> collect and answer reviews -> add 3-5 quality backlinks -> re-run PageSpeed.

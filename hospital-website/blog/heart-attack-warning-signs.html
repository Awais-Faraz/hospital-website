Heart attack warning signs and when to go to the ER

Published 2026-10-05 · CareNova Hospital medical team

A heart attack happens when blood flow to part of the heart is blocked. Treatment works best when it starts early, so knowing the signs can save a life.

Common warning signs

  • Pressure, tightness or heaviness in the centre of the chest
  • Pain spreading to the left arm, jaw, neck or back
  • Shortness of breath
  • Cold sweat, nausea or light-headedness

Signs that are easy to miss

Women, older adults and people with diabetes may have less typical symptoms, such as unusual tiredness, indigestion-like discomfort, or pain in the back or jaw without chest pain.

What to do

  • Call the emergency number or an ambulance straight away.
  • Do not drive yourself. Sit down and stay calm.
  • Take aspirin only if a doctor has told you it is safe for you.
  • Tell the team when symptoms started.

Lower your risk

Control blood pressure and sugar, stop smoking, stay active, eat less salt and fried food, and have regular check-ups after the age of 40.

Frequently asked questions

What are your emergency and OPD timings?

Open 24 hours, 7 days a week. OPD: Mon-Sat 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM.

How can I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this website, call us, or send a WhatsApp message. We will confirm your slot by phone.

Do you accept insurance and panel cards?

Yes. We work with several insurance companies and corporate panels. Please call our reception desk to confirm yours.

Can I get my lab reports online?

Yes. Reports can be collected from the lab or sent to you by WhatsApp or email.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available for patients and visitors.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your CNIC, previous reports, a list of current medicines, and your insurance card if you have one.

Need to see a specialist?

Book online in a minute, or call our reception. Emergency care needs no appointment.

About this department

Orthopedic surgeons and physiotherapists work together so that treatment does not stop at the operating table. We treat injuries, arthritis, spine and joint problems for all ages.

Conditions we treat

  • Fractures and dislocations
  • Knee and hip pain
  • Arthritis
  • Back and neck pain
  • Sports injuries
  • Joint replacement surgery

Tests and services

  • Digital X-ray
  • Ultrasound
  • MRI referral
  • Physiotherapy assessment
When to see a doctor: Pain that lasts more than two weeks, a joint that swells or locks, a limb you cannot put weight on, or visible deformity after an injury.

Frequently asked questions

Is physiotherapy available at the hospital?

Yes. A physiotherapist can start your rehabilitation plan before and after surgery.

When is surgery really needed?

Many problems improve with medicines, exercise and physiotherapy. Your surgeon will explain all options before recommending surgery.

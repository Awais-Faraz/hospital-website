About this department

From your first antenatal visit to delivery and postnatal care, our gynecologists and midwives follow your pregnancy closely. We also treat period problems, fertility concerns and menopause symptoms.

Conditions we treat

  • Antenatal and postnatal care
  • Normal and C-section delivery
  • Irregular or painful periods
  • Fertility assessment
  • PCOS
  • Menopause care

Tests and services

  • Pregnancy ultrasound
  • Fetal monitoring
  • Pap smear
  • Hormone profile
When to see a doctor: Missed period with pain, bleeding during pregnancy, reduced baby movements, very heavy periods, or pelvic pain.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have female doctors?

Yes. Female gynecologists and midwives are available during OPD and for deliveries.

Should I register early in pregnancy?

Yes. Early registration lets us plan scans and tests at the right time.

About this department

Our cardiology team looks after adults with chest pain, high blood pressure, heart failure, rhythm problems and coronary artery disease. Tests are done in-house so you do not have to run between labs.

Conditions we treat

  • Chest pain and angina
  • High blood pressure
  • Heart failure
  • Irregular heartbeat (arrhythmia)
  • Coronary artery disease
  • Post heart-attack follow-up

Tests and services

  • ECG and exercise stress test
  • Echocardiography
  • Holter (24-hour) monitoring
  • Cardiac blood tests
When to see a doctor: Chest pressure or pain, breathlessness on mild effort, fainting, a racing or very slow heartbeat, or swelling in both legs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an ECG without an appointment?

Yes. ECG is available during OPD hours and 24/7 in the emergency department.

Do I need a referral to see a cardiologist?

No referral is needed. Bring any previous reports and a list of your medicines.

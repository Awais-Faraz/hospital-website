Fever in children: when to see a doctor

Published 2026-10-18 · CareNova Hospital medical team

Fever is the body's way of fighting infection. The number on the thermometer matters less than how your child looks and behaves.

See a doctor immediately if

  • Your baby is under 3 months and has a temperature of 38°C (100.4°F) or higher
  • Your child is breathing fast or with difficulty
  • There is a stiff neck, a seizure, or a rash that does not fade when pressed
  • Your child is very sleepy, hard to wake, or not drinking

Book a visit if

  • Fever lasts more than 3 days
  • There are signs of dehydration: dry mouth, no tears, fewer wet nappies
  • Your child has an ear, throat or urine complaint

Care at home

Offer plenty of fluids, dress your child in light clothes and let them rest. Use fever medicine only in the dose advised by your doctor or pharmacist for your child's weight. Do not give aspirin to children.
